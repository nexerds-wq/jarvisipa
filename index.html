⌁ LONG-TERM STOCK RESEARCH RADAR

HISTORICAL DATA ONLY

Compare 1-year trends, 50/200-day moving averages, 3-month return and RSI. A BUY WATCH means the formula looks strong historically, not that you should buy. SELL / AVOID means the trend looks weak, not a guaranteed forecast. News and policy changes should be checked separately.

Live historical data uses an unofficial Yahoo Finance endpoint through third-party public CORS proxies, which may fail. No price or recommendation is shown if the data cannot be verified. Research only, not investment advice.

📈 HOW THIS RATING IS MADE

+1 price above 200-day average; +1 50-day above 200-day; +1 price higher than 3 months ago; +1 RSI 40–70; +1 positive 12-month performance. At least 200 real trading-day prices are required.

Scores: 4–5 BUY WATCH, 2–3 HOLD / REVIEW, 0–1 SELL / AVOID. This simple model does not use valuation, balance sheets or future news and is not a tested profitable system.
