SOLOS / IPHONE
CHECKING

Your iPhone.
Your glasses.

No IPA or internet required. Your Android phone handles the RFCOMM connection.

--:--
---

1. On Android, switch on Mobile Hotspot and connect the Solos glasses with the bridge app.
2. On iPhone, join the Android hotspot in Wi-Fi Settings.
3. Open this page using the address shown in the Android app. Tap the Send buttons.

For voice-to-text, use the microphone on the iPhone keyboard. The Android app must remain open, and this page must stay open for live clock updates.
